# Backlog

Approved unfinished work only.

Priority labels:

- **P0** - urgent correctness, safety, or install/update trust.
- **P1** - important product or agent-workflow improvement.
- **P2** - useful follow-up after higher-priority work.

Use priority headings for approved backlog items:

```markdown
## P1 - Short Task Name

**What:** concrete unfinished work.
**Why now:** why this belongs in backlog now.
**Expected gain:** what improves when it is done.
**Acceptance:** how the owner and agent know it is complete.
**Links:** related files, issues, chats, ideas, or ADRs.
```
